**Last updated:** January 2026

CSVN — Corporate Services Vendor Network ("CSVN", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Business name, tagline, and description
- Contact details (phone, WhatsApp, email, website)
- Business address and location
- Services offered
- Payment information (processed securely via our payment provider)

### Information Collected Automatically
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited
- Time and date of visits
- IP address
- Device information

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Display your listing on CSVN
- Connect you with potential buyers
- Process payments
- Send transactional emails
- Improve our platform
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. What We Do NOT Do

- ❌ We do **not** sell your personal information
- ❌ We do **not** share your contact details with third-party advertisers
- ❌ We do **not** use your data for anything other than CSVN's services

## 4. Public Information

Vendor listings are **publicly visible**. This includes:
- Business name
- Category, city, and area
- Contact information you provide
- Services offered
- About description

**Do not submit information you want to keep private.**

## 5. Cookies

CSVN uses cookies for:
- Session management
- Analytics (aggregate, anonymous)
- Preference storage

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

## 6. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **Payment processors** for UPI transactions
- **Hosting providers** (GitHub Pages)
- **Analytics tools** (aggregate usage data)
- **YouTube** for embedded videos

Each has its own privacy policy.

## 7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 8. Data Retention

- Active listings: retained while your listing is active
- After termination: contact us to request deletion
- Financial records: retained as required by law

## 9. Your Rights

You may:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion (subject to legal obligations)
- Opt out of marketing emails

To exercise these rights, email **info@csvn.in**.

## 10. Children's Privacy

CSVN is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of CSVN constitutes acceptance.
