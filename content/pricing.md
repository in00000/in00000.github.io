## Fair Rotation & Network Guarantee

**Auto-rotation policy.** To ensure fairness and consistent visibility for all featured partners, Featured and VIP listings are powered by an automated priority rotation system across the homepage, category pages, and monthly email digests. No listing is permanently buried.

- **Standard rotation (Founding Entry)** — your listing rotates across all Standard-tier listings in your category.
- **Top 3 Pool (Founding Featured)** — your listing rotates within the top 3 positions of your category page on every visit.
- **Homepage slider + Top 3 Pool (Founding VIP)** — your listing rotates within the top 3 positions of your category page and is included in the homepage featured slider rotation.

**Direct connection, zero commission.** CSVN is a direct media reach network. We charge **zero commission** on closed deals. Every enquiry connects directly to your business phone, WhatsApp, or website — we never sit in the middle of your transaction.

## What Every Plan Includes

Regardless of tier, every CSVN listing — now and forever — includes:

- **Direct contact details on your profile** — buyers reach out to you directly
- **No commission on deals** — all commercial terms are between you and the buyer
- **Rotation within your tier** — no listing is permanently buried
- **Payment receipt** for your records
- **Free listing edits** — email us when your details change
- **Removal anytime** — no lock-in, no cancellation fee
- **No cap on how many buyers can contact you** through the platform
- **Listing activation within 4 business days** of payment verification

Digest inclusion is a promotional placement. It does not guarantee opens, clicks, or enquiries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the Founding Round?**
It is our first and only discounted pricing. We are onboarding 70 vendors total — 50 Entry, 15 Featured, 5 VIP — at founder's rates before standard pricing opens. When the slots are gone, these prices are permanently closed.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
No. The Founding Entry plan is ₹3,999 for 5 full years — about ₹67 per month. It is the lowest price CSVN will ever offer.

**Q: Will I get enquiries?**
We can’t guarantee enquiries — that depends on buyer demand, your category, and your profile quality. What we provide is a professional listing on the CSVN directory, visible to buyers browsing the platform, plus rotation and promotional placement depending on your plan. Every listing rotates for fair placement within its pool.

**Q: What’s the difference between the rotation pools?**
Founding Entry listings rotate across all Standard-tier listings in a category. Founding Featured and Founding VIP listings rotate within the **Top 3** positions of the category page. Founding VIP listings are additionally included in the homepage featured slider and the monthly HR & Decision-Maker Digest.

**Q: Why is Founding Featured (3 years) more expensive than Founding Entry (5 years)?**
Because you're paying for visibility, not time. Founding Featured guarantees rotation within the top 3 positions of your category — that prime placement is worth more than two extra years at the bottom of the list. Founding Entry is for vendors who want a long runway; Founding Featured and VIP are for vendors who want prime position now.

**Q: Do I need GST to list?**
No. No plan requires GST. We’ll issue a simple payment receipt. If you have GST, you can provide it and we’ll include it in your receipt.

**Q: Can I upgrade later?**
Yes — you can upgrade from Founding Entry to Founding Featured or Founding VIP at any time by paying the difference. See [Upgrading Your Plan](#upgrading-your-plan) above.

**Q: Do you take a cut of my deals?**
No. CSVN does not charge commission, processing fees, or transaction fees on any plan. Whatever you charge your client, you keep.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
Send us your business details. We review them (1–2 business days). After payment verification, your listing goes live within **4 business days**. If you’re upgrading, the new placement and rotation benefits apply within the same window.

**Q: How do I pay ₹3,999, ₹6,999, or ₹9,999?**
UPI works for all amounts. For Founding Featured and Founding VIP, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) is also available on request — email us and we’ll send you the account details.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes — email us anytime and we’ll remove your listing within **4 business days**. Refunds are governed by our [Refund Policy](/refund/).

**Q: What happens when the Founding Round closes?**
Standard pricing opens. The exact standard pricing will be published when the last slot sells. Founding vendors keep the price they paid for the full term of their plan — no renewals at the founding price, but no price increase mid-term either.
