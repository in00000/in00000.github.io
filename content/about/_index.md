CSVN — **Corporate Services Vendor Network** — is an India-focused B2B visibility and promotion network connecting corporate service providers with the businesses that need them.

We are independently operated from **Pune, Maharashtra**.

## Our Mission

Make it easier for service providers to be discovered by decision-makers, and easier for businesses to find the right vendor.

## Our Vision

A transparent, commission-free platform where every vendor gets fair visibility and every buyer finds reliable contacts.

## Our Values

- **Honesty** — We say what we do, and we do what we say.
- **Transparency** — Clear pricing, clear terms, no hidden fees.
- **Fairness** — Every vendor gets equal rotation within their tier.
- **Directness** — No middlemen, no lead brokers, no commissions.

## What We Stand For

Every CSVN listing reflects exactly what the vendor chooses to publish — clear, accurate, and trustworthy. Listings rotate within their tier so every vendor enjoys equitable visibility.

We are transparent about how our platform works: **CSVN delivers visibility and promotional reach**, putting your business in front of decision-makers across India.

## Reach & Coverage

- **91 categories** across 12 service groups
- **90+ cities** and 100+ industrial belts
- **Pan-India** decision-maker network
