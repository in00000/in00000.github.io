First select your plan above. Then click the "Proceed to Secure Payment" button. You'll be redirected to UroPay's secure checkout where you can pay via UPI, Card, or Netbanking. Enter the amount matching your chosen plan.

Yes. You can upgrade to a higher founding plan at any time by paying the difference. To move to a lower plan, contact us at info@csvn.in and we'll adjust it at your next renewal.

Founding Entry is for vendors who want a long runway at standard placement; Featured and VIP are for vendors who want prime position now.
